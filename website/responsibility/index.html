Home vs Away

Algorithm to infer marking responsibilities

This is my own algorithm, developed from analyzing the standard marking methods and incorporating zone coverage. It works as follows:

  1. Find who can reach the ball first (and who can contest them shortly after).
  2. Build a near-ball region from those players and their Delaunay teammates, then expand it.
  3. Assign marking edges to attackers inside that region (oriented mutual 2-Nearest-Neighbours).
  4. Outside the bubble, colour each defender’s pitch-control space, corresponding to the zone they are responsible for covering.

Click a defender or their coloured zone to outline that space and highlight their marking edge(s).
